Our end-to-end approach to data quality
Potentia delivers high-quality research through a structured quality framework that combines expert project management, robust quality checks and trusted sample sources. Quality is built into every stage, supported by clear processes, project checklists and continuous feedback with clients and suppliers.

KEY PILLARS
End-to-End Quality Control
• Quality embedded at every stage (design → fieldwork → delivery)

• Two-step checks across all processes

• Project checklists ensure consistency, compliance, and auditability

• Quality is built into the full lifecycle, not added at the end
Strong Questionnaire Design
• AI-powered Survey Intelligence tool designed to strengthen questionnaires and optimise the respondent journey

• Reviewed by experienced PMs for clarity & simplicity, no bias / leading questions, and logical structure — avoiding overly long / repetitive surveys and respondent fatigue

• Better surveys drive better data quality
Duplicate Prevention
• Prevents multiple entries via unique IDs, cookies and additional checks where needed

• Ensures one respondent = one response
Fieldwork Monitoring
• Checked daily during fieldwork: interview length, dropout rates, incidence and quotas

• Enables real-time adjustments

• Issues are identified and resolved early
Data Validation
• Real-time fraud detection and duplicate prevention identify suspicious activity before respondents enter the survey

• Daily automated and manual quality checks monitor live responses throughout fieldwork

• Open-ended responses are reviewed to measure engagement and validate response quality

• Low-quality, irrelevant, duplicated or suspicious responses are removed, alongside inconsistent answer patterns where required

• Combining automated technology with experienced human review helps ensure only genuine, engaged respondents are included — protecting the integrity of the final dataset
Respondent Verification
• Alongside our manual quality processes, we use Alias and Verisoul to verify respondent identity, prevent duplicate participation, and detect fraudulent behaviour in real time

• Giving our clients greater confidence in the quality of the data we deliver
Hands-On Project Management
• PMs oversee all stages: questionnaire → scripting → delivery

• Key controls: clear ownership, all changes tracked & verified, close client alignment

• Strong control reduces errors and risk
Scripting & Translation Quality
• Scripting: full pre-launch testing — logic, routing, quotas. Internal review before the client sees links

• Translation: dual-layer review (PM + agency) with checks for accuracy, formatting, completeness

• Nothing goes live without full validation
Sample & Supplier Control
• Only trusted, vetted suppliers

• New suppliers tested before scaling; performance tracked per project

• Poor-quality sources removed

• Includes proprietary panels and specialist validated audiences

• Quality starts with who you survey
Flexible / Custom Approach
• Tailored quality controls depending on sample source (client vs Potentia), including email validation (identifies invalid contacts) and custom quality rules

• Adaptable but still controlled
Data Processing & Output Quality
• Structured workflow: interim outputs for early issue detection, final validation before delivery, with PM + data team both reviewing

• Outputs are verified, not assumed
Continuous Improvement
• Regular team training, process reviews, and client feedback surveys

• Ongoing refinement of methods, tools, and supplier strategy

• Always evolving to maintain high standards

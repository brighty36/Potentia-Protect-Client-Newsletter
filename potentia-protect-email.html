Questions about research quality are changing. Verification, duplicate respondents, AI-generated answers and sample provenance are increasingly part of the conversation. When someone asks how you know your research data is real, you need a better answer than “we trust our sample supplier”.
You need clear answers, especially when those questions come from colleagues, procurement teams or end clients. So here are ours.
How do you know a respondent hasn't taken this survey three times?
At Potentia, we run de-duplication across every sample source, so if a respondent is in three panels, they still only reach your survey once. We back this up with unique IDs, cookies, and device fingerprinting, and our partners at Verisoul screen for VPN and proxy use, account linking, and geolocation mismatches before anyone enters the survey.
 
What happens when AI writes an open end?
We catch this twice. Our tools, Alias and Aftercare, score open ends in real time for nonsense, low effort, inconsistency, and LLM-generated content. Then, one of our project managers reads every open end daily, looking for context mismatches and patterns the tools might miss. We remove and reconcile anything that fails, so you can trust the responses you receive.
 
How do you vet who is in the sample?
Every supplier goes through our formal onboarding process and is assessed against ISO 20252 and the ESOMAR 37. We track performance project by project, and we drop underperforming sources. Our own panels sit alongside specialist validated audiences, so you benefit from our direct oversight as well as trusted external sources.
 
What if something is wrong while we are still in field?
Our soft launch analytics flag anomalies in interview length, incidence, drop-out rates, and quota behaviour before the study scales. We continue daily checks throughout fieldwork, so we can fix any problems while there is still time.
 
Who checks the data before it reaches us?
Wherever timing allows, we provide interim tables so issues surface before fieldwork closes. Final outputs are reviewed by both your project manager and our data team. SigDiff, our in-house analytics group, can run a meta-analysis across your dataset as a further layer before release, giving you extra confidence in the results.
 
How is our own data held?
We have a dedicated Data Protection Officer and a Compliance Director, and we are ISO 27001 certified through PJR. Our team handles all client samples, personal data, and sensitive information in line with GDPR and the relevant local legislation in the markets we operate in. Your data privacy is always our priority.

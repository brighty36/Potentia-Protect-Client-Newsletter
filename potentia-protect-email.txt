Our end-to-end approach to data quality

====================================================================
KEY PILLARS
====================================================================

END-TO-END QUALITY CONTROL
- Quality embedded at every stage (design > fieldwork > delivery)
- Two-step checks across all processes
- Project checklists ensure consistency, compliance, and auditability
- Quality is built into the full lifecycle, not added at the end

HANDS-ON PROJECT MANAGEMENT
- PMs oversee all stages: questionnaire > scripting > delivery
- Key controls: clear ownership, all changes tracked & verified, close
  client alignment
- Strong control reduces errors and risk

STRONG QUESTIONNAIRE DESIGN
- AI-powered Survey Intelligence tool designed to strengthen
  questionnaires and optimise the respondent journey
- Reviewed by experienced PMs for clarity & simplicity, no bias /
  leading questions, and logical structure - avoiding overly long /
  repetitive surveys and respondent fatigue
- Better surveys drive better data quality

SCRIPTING & TRANSLATION QUALITY
- Scripting: full pre-launch testing - logic, routing, quotas. Internal
  review before the client sees links
- Translation: dual-layer review (PM + agency) with checks for accuracy,
  formatting, completeness
- Nothing goes live without full validation

DUPLICATE PREVENTION
- Prevents multiple entries via unique IDs, cookies and additional
  checks where needed
- Ensures one respondent = one response

SAMPLE & SUPPLIER CONTROL
- Only trusted, vetted suppliers
- New suppliers tested before scaling; performance tracked per project
- Poor-quality sources removed
- Includes proprietary panels and specialist validated audiences
- Quality starts with who you survey

FIELDWORK MONITORING
- Checked daily during fieldwork: interview length, dropout rates,
  incidence and quotas
- Enables real-time adjustments
- Issues are identified and resolved early

DATA VALIDATION
- Real-time fraud detection and duplicate prevention identify suspicious
  activity before respondents enter the survey
- Daily automated and manual quality checks monitor live responses
  throughout fieldwork
- Open-ended responses are reviewed to measure engagement and validate
  response quality
- Low-quality, irrelevant, duplicated or suspicious responses are
  removed, alongside inconsistent answer patterns where required
- Combining automated technology with experienced human review helps
  ensure only genuine, engaged respondents are included - protecting the
  integrity of the final dataset

FLEXIBLE / CUSTOM APPROACH
- Tailored quality controls depending on sample source (client vs
  Potentia), including email validation (identifies invalid contacts)
  and custom quality rules
- Adaptable but still controlled

DATA PROCESSING & OUTPUT QUALITY
- Structured workflow: interim outputs for early issue detection, final
  validation before delivery, with PM + data team both reviewing
- Outputs are verified, not assumed

CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
- Regular team training, process reviews, and client feedback surveys
- Ongoing refinement of methods, tools, and supplier strategy
- Always evolving to maintain high standards

RESPONDENT VERIFICATION
- Alongside our manual quality processes, we use Alias and Verisoul to
  verify respondent identity, prevent duplicate participation, and detect
  fraudulent behaviour in real time
- Giving our clients greater confidence in the quality of the data we
  deliver

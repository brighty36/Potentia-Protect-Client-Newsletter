Subject: How do we know this data is real?
Preheader: Before you scope your Q4 studies, the answers worth having to hand.

POTENTIA PROTECT
How we're tackling your biggest data quality concerns

Questions about research quality are changing. Verification, duplicate
respondents, AI-generated answers and sample provenance are increasingly
part of the conversation. When someone asks how you know your research
data is real, you need a better answer than "we trust our sample
supplier".

You need clear answers, especially when those questions come from
colleagues, procurement teams or end clients. So here are ours.


HOW DO YOU KNOW A RESPONDENT HASN'T TAKEN THIS SURVEY THREE TIMES?
At Potentia, we run de-duplication across every sample source, so if a
respondent is in three panels, they still only reach your survey once. We
back this up with unique IDs, cookies, and device fingerprinting, and our
partners at Alias and Verisoul screen for VPN and proxy use, account
linking, and geolocation mismatches before anyone enters the survey.

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN AI WRITES AN OPEN END?
We catch this in two ways. Real-time automated checks and daily manual
review both feed into how we assess every open end, looking at relevance,
depth, consistency and overall quality. Low-quality, irrelevant or
inconsistent responses are removed, so you can trust the responses you
receive.

HOW DO YOU VET WHO IS IN THE SAMPLE?
Every supplier goes through our formal onboarding process and is assessed
against ISO 20252 and the ESOMAR 37. We track performance project by
project, and we drop underperforming sources. Our own panels sit
alongside specialist validated audiences, so you benefit from our direct
oversight as well as trusted external sources.

WHAT IF SOMETHING IS WRONG WHILE WE ARE STILL IN FIELD?
Our soft launch analytics flag anomalies in interview length, incidence,
drop-out rates, and quota behaviour before the study scales. We continue
daily checks throughout fieldwork, so we can fix any problems while there
is still time.

WHO CHECKS THE DATA BEFORE IT REACHES US?
Wherever timing allows, we provide interim tables so issues surface
before fieldwork closes. Final outputs are reviewed by both your project
manager and our data team. SigDiff, our in-house analytics group, can run
a meta-analysis across your dataset as a further layer before release,
giving you extra confidence in the results.

HOW IS OUR OWN DATA HELD?
We have a dedicated Data Protection Officer and a Compliance Director,
and we are ISO 27001 certified through PJR. Our team handles all client
samples, personal data, and sensitive information in line with GDPR and
the relevant local legislation in the markets we operate in. Your data
privacy is always our priority.

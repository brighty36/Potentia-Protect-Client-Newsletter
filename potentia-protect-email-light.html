Your data quality questions, answered
September usually means scoping the next round of work. It also means fielding harder questions than you were getting a year ago, about where respondents come from, how you know they are human, and what happens to a survey answer written by a chatbot.
Those are fair questions and you should not have to guess at the answers. Here are ours, in a form you can forward to whoever is asking.
How do you know a respondent hasn't taken this survey three times?
De-duplication runs across every sample source at once, not source by source. So a respondent who belongs to three panels still only reaches your survey once. Unique IDs, cookies and device fingerprinting back it up, and Verisoul screens for VPN and proxy use, account linking and geolocation mismatches before anyone enters the survey.
 
 
What happens when an open end is written by AI?
It gets caught twice. Alias and Aftercare score open ends in real time for nonsense, low effort, inconsistency and LLM-generated content. Then a project manager reads every open end, every day, looking for context mismatches and patterns the tools miss. Anything that fails is removed and reconciled.
 
 
How do you vet who is in the sample?
Every supplier goes through formal onboarding and is assessed against ISO 20252 and the ESOMAR 37. Performance is tracked project by project, and sources that underperform are dropped rather than tolerated. Our own panels sit alongside specialist validated audiences.
 
 
What if something is wrong while we are still in field?
Soft launch analytics flag anomalies in length of interview, incidence, drop-out and quota behaviour before the study scales. Daily checks continue throughout fieldwork, so problems get fixed while there is still time to fix them rather than explained afterwards.
 
 
Who checks the data before it reaches us?
Interim tables wherever timing allows, so issues surface before fieldwork closes. Final outputs are reviewed by both your project manager and the data team. SigDiff, our in-house analytics team, can run meta-analysis across the dataset as a further layer before release.
 
 
How is our own data held?
We have a dedicated Data Protection Officer and a Compliance Director, and we are ISO 27001 certified through PJR. All client samples, personal data and sensitive information are handled in line with GDPR and the relevant local legislation in every market we field in.
